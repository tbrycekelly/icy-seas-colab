# Build your research at Icy Seas

Icy Seas Co-Laboratory is a private research institute based in Fairbanks, Alaska. We welcome scientists who want to pursue collaborative research, develop new methods, and connect their science with practical tools and open, reproducible workflows.

<div class="portfolio-callout">
<h2>No current unfilled positions</h2>
<p>We are always reviewing applications for staff scientist positions. Applications are considered on an ongoing basis as we explore scientific fit, funding, and opportunities to develop a research program together.</p>
</div>

## Staff scientist appointments

Our research connects oceanography, marine biogeochemistry, environmental observations, imaging, scientific computing, and research data systems. We are interested in hearing from scientists whose work complements or extends these areas.

Staff scientist positions are **soft-money appointments**: salary support depends on grants, contracts, or other project funding. **There is no guaranteed salary independent of available funding.** The funding plan and supported effort are central to discussions about an appointment.

We offer benefits and higher starting salaries than comparable university positions. Specific compensation, benefits, and appointment terms are discussed individually in relation to the proposed role and available funding.

## Developing and bringing research funding

We can support proposal development and help scientists plan a research program at Icy Seas. You are welcome to get in touch with a proposal idea, work already in development, or existing research support.

Where possible, we can negotiate the transfer of current awards to Icy Seas. Transfers depend on sponsor requirements and agreement among the organizations involved; we review what is feasible for each award.

## How to apply

Email **[admin@icyseascolab.io](mailto:admin@icyseascolab.io?subject=Staff%20scientist%20application)** with the subject **Staff scientist application** and include:

- **Cover letter:** Introduce yourself and explain your interest in joining Icy Seas.
- **CV or résumé:** Describe your research experience, skills, and contributions.
- **Research narrative (up to three pages):** Pitch the research you would pursue at Icy Seas. Explain why it is fundable, how it aligns with [our goals and research priorities](/research/), and what makes it exciting. Describe potential funding sources, proposals in development, or existing awards where relevant.

You may also include links to relevant publications, software, datasets, or other research outputs.

An initial conversation can help us assess research fit, discuss salary and benefits, and explore a realistic funding plan.

## Working together

Our approach values scientific curiosity, collaboration, autonomy, and respect for both data and people. We welcome applicants from all backgrounds who share our commitment to transparent and reproducible science.

Learn about our [research](/research/), [meet the team](/member/), or review our [equal employment opportunity commitments](/docs/business_policies/#equal-employment-opportunity-eeo).
