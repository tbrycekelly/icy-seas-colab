abstract = "This study evaluated a carbon-based primary-production model using continuous shipboard optical observations in the northern Gulf of Alaska. The model performed reasonably across waters with differing optical properties, including waters influenced by freshwater inputs. Underway measurements resolved sharp productivity gradients associated with ocean fronts that conventional station sampling can miss. The work connects shipboard observing systems with the fine-scale ecological patterns needed to interpret a dynamic coastal ocean."
