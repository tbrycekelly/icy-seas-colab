abstract = "Using 21 years of satellite chlorophyll observations from 2003–2023, this study classified Gulf of Alaska phytoplankton seasons into double-bloom, single spring-bloom, single fall-bloom, and no-bloom regimes. Cold, dynamically active years tended to support distinct spring and fall blooms, while warm, weakly forced conditions favored single spring blooms or no distinct bloom. Marine heatwave years were associated with a reduced double-bloom footprint. The analysis found no significant long-term trend in the area occupied by each regime, emphasizing strong year-to-year reorganization rather than a persistent directional shift over the study period."
