abstract = "The BLOOFINZ/INDITUN collaboration investigated the spawning habitat of southern bluefin tuna off northwest Australia during January–March 2022. Continuous underway observations and four multi-day Lagrangian experiments connected nutrient supply, plankton production, grazing, carbon export, and larval feeding and growth. The synthesis describes how food-web structure supports tuna larvae in nutrient-poor waters and compares this habitat with the spawning region of Atlantic bluefin tuna in the Gulf of Mexico. It illustrates the value of coordinated studies that connect ocean physics and biogeochemistry with fisheries ecology."
