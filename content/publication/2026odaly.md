abstract = "Underwater particle imaging along two Southern Ocean transects revealed contrasting routes by which biological material reaches the deep ocean. Deep-learning features and clustering helped classify millions of images into living organisms and detrital particle types. One pathway was associated with abundant fluffy aggregates extending into deep water; another involved mesopelagic grazers and the production of dense particles and fecal material. The findings connect particle identity and zooplankton activity with carbon storage, showing why biological transformations below the sunlit ocean matter for interpreting carbon export."
