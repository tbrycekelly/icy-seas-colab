abstract = "Following a drifting water parcel helps researchers separate biological change from the movement of water. This study examined four Lagrangian experiments off northwest Australia and accounted for the vertical displacement of density layers. Comparing temperature, salinity, chlorophyll, and oxygen along density surfaces reduced the apparent variability seen at fixed depths and supported the interpretation that each experiment followed a consistent water parcel. The method helps distinguish changes within the ecosystem from changes caused by internal waves and vertical motion."
