The TAG

The Trace Analysis Group at the University of Alaska Fairbanks’ International Arctic Research Center brings together researchers and technical partners to measure trace elements and nutrients in challenging environmental samples. Its work connects clean sample handling, analytical methods, quality control, and data stewardship across ocean, sea-ice, river, and terrestrial research.

Thomas supports The TAG through Icy Seas’ scientific software, laboratory systems, and reproducible data workflows.
